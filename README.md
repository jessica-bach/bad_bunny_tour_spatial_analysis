# 🎵 Debí Tirar Más Fotos Tour

## Mobility, Identity, and Territory: A Spatial Analysis of Bad Bunny’s World Tour

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Debí Tirar Más Fotos Tour** by Puerto Rican artist **Bad Bunny** using Geographic Information Systems (GIS), spatial databases, and geospatial analysis techniques.

Based on the official tour schedule, cities, and venues announced for the tour, a spatial database was developed in **PostgreSQL/PostGIS** to examine the geographical distribution of concerts, the artist's mobility patterns, audience concentration, and selected environmental impacts associated with the global itinerary.

The project combines **SQL**, **PostGIS**, and **QGIS** to transform basic event information into spatial indicators capable of explaining patterns related to language, city size, market penetration, and the carbon footprint generated by international travel.

---

## Objectives

- Build a spatial database of the tour.
- Analyze the geographical distribution of concerts.
- Study the relationship between language and concert locations.
- Evaluate the influence of city size on the number of performances.
- Estimate the territorial distribution of attendees.
- Reconstruct the complete tour itinerary.
- Calculate travel distances, flight times, and CO₂ emissions.
- Produce thematic maps and visualizations to communicate findings.

## Main Analyses

### Concert Distribution

Assessment of the tour's global geography and the concentration of performances by city and country.

### Per-Capita Attendance

Estimation of audience size by city and calculation of per-capita indicators to compare markets of different population sizes.

### International Mobility

Spatial reconstruction of the tour itinerary through the **tour_lines** layer, connecting consecutive destinations and enabling the calculation of travel distances, flight durations, and carbon emissions.

### Environmental Impact

Estimation of travel distances, flight times, and CO₂ emissions for each segment of the tour.

### Cross-Country Comparison

Calculation of each country's share of total estimated attendees worldwide.

## Included Geographic Layers

| Layer | Geometry Type | Description |
|---------|---------|---------|
| stadiums.gpkg | Point | Host cities with demographic, attendance, and operational attributes |
| tour_lines.gpkg | Line | Chronological flight segments connecting consecutive tour destinations |
| tour_countries.gpkg | Polygon | Countries included in the tour, enriched with aggregated attendance indicators |
| world_countries.gpkg | Polygon | Global reference layer used for cartographic production and spatial analysis |

Each layer includes a dedicated metadata document describing data sources, processing methods, data quality, and a complete attribute inventory.

## Key Findings

- Spanish-speaking territories account for the majority of concerts on the tour.
- Spain and Mexico together represent more than 40% of total estimated attendees.
- San Juan (Puerto Rico) exhibits the highest attendance relative to its population.
- Lisbon, Arnhem, and Düsseldorf show per-capita attendance levels comparable to those of Puerto Rico.
- The tour covers approximately 70,000 kilometers.
- The Sydney-Tokyo route generated the highest carbon footprint per attendee.
- The Lisbon-Madrid route was the most environmentally efficient segment of the tour.
- Language appears to be a stronger explanatory factor than population size in the spatial organization of the tour.
